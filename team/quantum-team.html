Clinical Supervisor

Dr. Lyz Amarillo

LCPC, LPC

Dr. Lyz Amarillo is a bilingual psychotherapist, clinical supervisor, virtual mental health consultant, and THRIVE evaluator with experience supporting diverse clients and communities.

View More

Dr. Lyz Amarillo is a seasoned mental health provider who has been making a meaningful impact in the field since 2008. As a bilingual psychotherapist licensed in Illinois, Texas, and Tennessee, Dr. Amarillo brings extensive knowledge, compassion, and experience to her practice. Her role as an evaluator for THRIVE further expands her contribution to behavioral health services.

Dr. Amarillo's professional experience spans a wide range of mental health settings, including work with domestic violence survivors, substance detoxification and residential programs, minors in residential treatment, psychiatric hospitals, crisis teams, and private practice.

Her career has been especially marked by her commitment to serving communities of color and individuals in high-risk areas, particularly in the city of Chicago.

She works with a diverse range of clients, including youth, adults, couples, families, and geriatric populations. Dr. Amarillo believes in the transformative power of psychotherapy and views therapy as a way to help individuals recognize their strengths, understand themselves more deeply, and embrace their authentic selves.

Her therapeutic approach is individualized and flexible. Dr. Amarillo uses an eclectic style of psychotherapy, recognizing that every person's life journey, circumstances, and needs are different. This approach allows her to create personalized treatment plans designed to provide each client with the support and guidance most appropriate for their goals.

A passionate advocate for both the mental health profession and the clients it serves, Dr. Amarillo is active in advocacy efforts at the state and federal levels. She is deeply committed to promoting conversations about mental health wellness within communities of color and reducing the stigma that can prevent people from seeking support.

Dr. Amarillo encourages clients to take an active role in their therapeutic journey, including choosing a psychotherapist who feels like the right fit. She places a strong emphasis on client autonomy, comfort, and success and, when appropriate, supports referrals to colleagues who may be better suited to a client's individual needs.

In her role as an evaluator for THRIVE, Dr. Amarillo brings her extensive clinical experience and insight to help ensure that programs and services remain effective, client-centered, responsive, and continuously improving.

Her work with THRIVE helps shape mental health services that resonate with and empower diverse communities.

Dr. Lyz Amarillo is more than a psychotherapist and evaluator; she is a guide, advocate, and dedicated mental health professional committed to the well-being, growth, and success of the individuals and communities she serves.

  • Bilingual psychotherapy
  • Clinical supervision
  • THRIVE evaluation

Behavioral Health Clinician

Dr. Ebonie Davis

Ed.D., LPC, NCC

Dr. Ebonie Davis supports adolescents, young adults, families, and school-based initiatives through a holistic, strengths-based approach grounded in empowerment, reflection, and accountability.

View More

Dr. Ebonie S. Davis, Ed.D., LPC, NCC, is a Licensed Professional Counselor whose work reflects both clinical skill and deep humanity. With nearly a decade of experience in education and clinical counseling, she has dedicated her career to helping adolescents and young adults navigate complex life transitions while developing the emotional and academic skills needed to thrive.

Her philosophy is grounded in the belief that when people feel seen, supported, and encouraged to believe in their own capacity, healing and growth can follow naturally.

Dr. Davis uses a holistic, strengths-based approach that draws from humanistic, cognitive-behavioral, and solution-focused frameworks. She views each client as more than a diagnosis or circumstance and takes time to understand their environment, relationships, values, strengths, and individual experiences.

Her sessions emphasize empowerment, reflection, and accountability, creating space for clients to explore who they are and who they want to become. Whether helping a young adult work through anxiety and self-doubt or supporting a family as they rebuild connection after conflict, her goal is to help people strengthen resilience, self-awareness, and confidence.

One experience that continues to influence Dr. Davis's work involved a high school student struggling academically after multiple suspensions and family instability. Rather than focusing solely on behavior correction, she collaborated with the student's teachers and family to identify his strengths and develop a plan centered on structure, emotional regulation, and achievable goals.

Within six months, the student's grades improved, attendance became more consistent, and he began mentoring younger peers. For Dr. Davis, the experience reinforced the importance of relational trust, individualized care, and recognizing potential rather than focusing only on challenges.

Dr. Davis also draws from her own experience as a first-generation college graduate. Her journey through higher education included moments of uncertainty and isolation that now inform her empathy for students and clients who may feel unseen in academic or professional environments.

Those experiences helped shape her belief that resilience is often strengthened through connection and community. She brings that perspective into her counseling work by modeling authenticity and encouraging clients to recognize the value and strength within their own lived experiences.

Dr. Davis earned her Doctorate in Higher Education with a concentration in Educational Practices, a Master's degree in Professional and School Counseling, and a Bachelor's degree in Social Work. This multidisciplinary background allows her to bridge education, psychology, counseling, and social systems in her clinical practice.

Throughout her career, she has provided individual counseling, crisis intervention, and program development for diverse populations across cultural and socioeconomic backgrounds.

Grounded in empathy, informed by evidence, and shaped by experience, Dr. Davis works to create spaces where clients feel safe enough to be honest, supported enough to explore difficult experiences, and challenged to continue growing.

She views therapy not only as a tool for relief, but also as a path toward discovering strength, purpose, resilience, and self-worth.

  • School-based support
  • Adolescents & young adults
  • Resilience building

Psychiatric Nurse Practitioner

Abidemi “Abby” Adewoyin

APRN, RN, PMHNP-BC

Abidemi “Abby” Adewoyin is a board-certified Psychiatric-Mental Health Nurse Practitioner who specializes in thoughtful, evidence-based medication management. She works with patients through evaluation, diagnosis, and prescribing when appropriate while making the treatment process clear, collaborative, and supportive.

View More

Starting psychiatric medication—or even considering it—can feel overwhelming. Abby works to make the process easier, clearer, and more comfortable. During appointments, she talks openly with patients about their symptoms, goals, and how medication may help, explaining treatment options in plain language and partnering with each person in making decisions that feel right for them.

Abby treats a wide range of mental health concerns, including depression, generalized anxiety, social anxiety, panic disorder, ADHD, bipolar disorder, trauma- and stressor-related conditions, PTSD, and other mood and behavioral health disorders.

She also supports patients who have tried multiple medications without success, with an emphasis on identifying effective treatment options while minimizing unwanted side effects whenever possible.

Abby cares deeply about making mental health care accessible and inclusive. She has a special passion for serving underserved and marginalized communities, including Black, Brown, LGBTQ+, and immigrant patients who may experience stigma or limited access to care.

Her approach is culturally responsive, trauma-informed, welcoming, and grounded in warmth, respect, privacy, and nonjudgmental support. Her goal is to help patients feel informed and comfortable as they work toward greater stability, clarity, balance, and hope.

Abby believes medication decisions should be made collaboratively. She works with each patient to create an individualized plan that fits their needs, circumstances, and treatment goals.

  • Medication management
  • Psychiatric evaluation
  • Anxiety & depression
  • ADHD & mood disorders
  • Trauma-informed care

Behavioral Health Clinician

Kendrick Jones

MS, PLPC

Kendrick Jones provides behavioral health services with a strong commitment to underserved communities, culturally sensitive care, intake support, and reducing barriers to mental health access.

View More

Hailing from St. Louis, Missouri, Kendrick Jones provides behavioral health services with a deep commitment to underserved communities. His work in mental health is not only a professional pursuit, but also a personal mission to bring meaningful support, healing, and greater access to those who need it most.

Born and raised in St. Louis, Kendrick's connection to his community runs deep. His understanding of local culture and community dynamics has helped shape his approach to mental health care and strengthened his sense of responsibility to give back to the city that helped shape him.

At Quantum, Kendrick is actively involved in supporting clients experiencing a range of mental health concerns, including anxiety, depression, and anger management. His approach is empathetic, client-centered, and focused on helping individuals feel understood, supported, and empowered throughout the therapeutic process.

Kendrick's responsibilities also extend beyond therapy sessions. He is often one of the first points of contact for new clients, assisting with intake sessions and helping establish a foundation of trust, professionalism, and genuine care from the beginning of the client experience.

His professional interests sit at the intersection of mental health and social justice. Kendrick is deeply committed to working with marginalized and underserved communities and recognizes the unique barriers these populations may face when seeking behavioral health services.

His goal is to provide culturally sensitive and inclusive counseling that addresses not only presenting symptoms, but also the broader social and environmental factors that can influence mental health and well-being.

Through his work, Kendrick hopes to help break down barriers to mental health care access and challenge the stigma that can prevent individuals—particularly those in underserved communities—from seeking support.

Kendrick envisions a future in which quality mental health care is accessible to individuals regardless of their background, circumstances, or community. His work at Quantum reflects that vision through compassion, advocacy, cultural awareness, and a commitment to meaningful client connection.

  • Anxiety & depression
  • Anger management
  • Intake support
  • Culturally responsive care

Project THRIVE

Hezekiah Williams

THRIVE Coordinator

Hezekiah Williams is a dynamic and passionate advocate in HIV awareness and support, particularly within Black and Brown LGBTQIA+ communities. With a decade of experience that began in his late teens as a volunteer at The SPOT, his work is rooted in advocacy, education, community building, and lived experience.

View More

Hezekiah's involvement in HIV advocacy began during his formative years. While volunteering at The SPOT, he gained invaluable experience and insight that helped shape his future path. As a Black, gay, HIV-positive man, Hezekiah understands the intersectional challenges and strengths of his community and uses his personal experiences to foster understanding and support for individuals living with HIV.

Hezekiah has been instrumental in creating and facilitating support groups, leadership meetings, and educational seminars. His initiatives focus on creating safe and inclusive spaces for open dialogue and community building.

He emphasizes the importance of healthy relationships between HIV-positive and HIV-negative individuals, challenging stigma, and fostering pride, acceptance, and connection within the community.

Currently pursuing a Bachelor of Arts in Psychology, Hezekiah's academic journey is closely aligned with his mission to uplift and empower his community from within. His educational goals are deeply connected to his commitment to becoming an even more effective leader and advocate.

In his role as THRIVE Coordinator, Hezekiah is at the forefront of coordinating activities and programs that directly impact the lives of the community he serves. He helps oversee center operations and works to ensure that services remain responsive, inclusive, and impactful.

Hezekiah is driven by a vision in which being HIV-positive is not a source of shame, but a call to resilience and solidarity. His leadership style is marked by empathy, inclusivity, and a deep understanding of the cultural nuances affecting his community. Through his work with THRIVE, he continues to break down barriers, build bridges, and champion HIV awareness and support within Black and Brown LGBTQIA+ communities.
